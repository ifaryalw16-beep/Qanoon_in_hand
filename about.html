About Qanoon24/7

We are on a mission to digitize Pakistan's legal system and make access to justice simple and easy for everyone.

Our Mission

Qanoon24/7 is more than just a portal; it is a mission to digitize Pakistan's legal system. By bringing the Public, Students, and Lawyers together, we are creating a complete "Legal Ecosystem."

For the Public: We explain complex laws in easy Urdu and regional languages so people can protect themselves from fraud and harassment.

For Students: We provide a practical platform for skill development that goes beyond just reading textbooks.

For Lawyers: We help them go paper-free by digitizing daily tasks like case management, hearing dates, and client handling.
